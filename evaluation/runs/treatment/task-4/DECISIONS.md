# Decisions

- System: 24x24 grid, 2-unit safe margin, one stroke weight (2px, round caps/joins),
  fill:none, currentColor. Keystones: 18-unit circle/gear field, 14x18 portrait doc, 16x18 cube.
- Corner language: r=2 arcs on axis-aligned frames (scan brackets, document); computed r=1.6
  tangent arcs on the alert triangle (acute corners read optically equal to r=2 right angles);
  round joins soften the cube and gear vertices.
- Gear and triangle geometry generated by script (8 teeth, outer r9/root r6.6, flank angles
  11.5/6.5 deg; true tangent-point rounding on the triangle), not eyeballed.
- Report = document + column chart to distinguish it from a generic file; bar pitch widened
  from 3 to 3.5 units after the 16px render showed the 1px gaps merging.
- Alert exclamation rebalanced (stem 9.6-13.2, dot 16.8) after render showed dot/stem crowding.
- preview.html inlines a copy of the symbol sheet because Chrome does not resolve external
  <use> references over file://; icons.svg remains the canonical sheet (with <title>s,
  role=img on the sheet, and a visible <use> reference row).
- Verified: XML parses (python ElementTree), no duplicate ids; headless Chrome renders of
  preview.html at 1x and 3x device scale, inspected at 16/24/48px on both the light and dark
  panels; 16px and 24px rows inspected at 3x zoom; defects above found and fixed, then
  re-rendered and re-inspected. Not verified: non-Chrome browsers, sub-16px sizes, printing.
