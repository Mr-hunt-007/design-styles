How Meridian works

From one domain seed to a ranked, evidence-backed work queue

01 · DISCOVERY

Discovery that never sleeps

Meridian walks outward from your domains the way an attacker would: certificates, DNS, registrars, cloud tenants, code hosts and marketplaces. Every asset is attributed to your organization with a confidence score before it enters your inventory.

  • Continuous re-enumeration, not quarterly snapshots
  • Shadow IT and forgotten subsidiaries surfaced automatically
  • Attribution evidence attached to every asset

02 · PRIORITIZATION

Ranked by consequence, not score dumps

  • Severity colors are reserved for severity, nothing else
  • Reachability verified from the internet, not assumed
  • One queue your engineers can work top to bottom

03 · EVIDENCE

Proof your engineers do not argue with

Every finding ships with the request that proved it, a freshness timestamp, and a one-click retest. When evidence goes stale, Meridian says so instead of pretending. Findings route to the owning team with the proof attached.

  • Reproducible evidence on every finding
  • Stale data is flagged, never silently reported as current
  • Routes to your ticketing, chat and SIEM tools
