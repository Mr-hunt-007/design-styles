# Candidate B: the technical drawing

**Direction:** the render-and-verify loop drawn as a FIG. 1 engineering-manual diagram on vellum, so the banner is itself a verified technical artefact of the skill it names.
**System:** warm paper (#F4F1E9) + near-black ink (#1F221E) in three stroke weights (2.5 circuit/nodes, 1.8 symbols, 1.2 annotation); all-mono system stack (ui-monospace/SF Mono/Menlo/Consolas fallbacks); four stations (make, render, inspect, fix) on a closed clockwise circuit; one vermilion accent (#C2401F) meaning exactly one thing: the fault the render exposes and its correction (defect in the render canvas, dot under the loupe, proofreader's caret); name and proposition lead from the left at 84/34px.
**Excluded:** blueprint/dark-cockpit colourways, dimension lines and fake measurements, terminal chrome, gradients and glow — anything that decorates the aerospace register instead of borrowing its discipline; accent never carries text, meaning never by colour alone.
**Risk:** the light card is bright against GitHub dark (checked, reads as a physical sheet); station sub-captions and footer are second-look detail that vanishes below ~640px by design.
**Verified:** XML valid; headless Chrome renders at 1280x640 (1x and 2x), 400px card on white and on #0d1117 with rounded corners, plus 3x zoom of the lower stations; one defect found and fixed from the zoom render (FIX caret off-centre, apex colliding with the ink line); name and proposition legible at 400px; all critical content inside the 64px safe margin; copy limited to README claims, no numbers, no logos, no em dashes.
